Project Description
CrowNav is a satirical navigation web app designed exclusively for birds. It completely rejects human road infrastructure, traffic laws, and municipal boundaries to plot unapologetic, laser-straight Euclidean vectors from origin to destination.

The Problem (that doesn't exist)
Birds are constantly getting lost because standard mapping apps force them to follow human roads, obey one-way streets, and respect private property lines. A pigeon shouldn't have to take the I-10 highway exit and wait at a red light just to get to an overflowing dumpster. Furthermore, current navigation apps severely lack critical avian telemetry, such as calculating the exact wing-flap cost of an Indian Peafowl trying to fly 500 kilometers.

The Solution (that nobody asked for)
CrowNav ignores all terrestrial geometry and draws a single, hardware-accelerated straight line from Point A to Point B. It calculates pure Haversine geodesic distance, tracks true compass bearings, and estimates total "Flap Cost" based on distinct aerodynamic profiles of native Indian birds. It also dynamically generates gritty, highly realistic pitstops along the vector line, ensuring birds can easily find muddy puddles, discarded street food, and dense canopy crevices along their migration route.
